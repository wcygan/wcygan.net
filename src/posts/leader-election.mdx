A [failure detector](/failure-detectors) tells a node when to suspect its leader has failed. [Raft's election algorithm](https://raft.github.io/raft.pdf#page=5) describes what happens next: the follower becomes a candidate, [starts a new term](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raft_(algorithm)#:~:text=In%20this%20case,a%20new%20election.>) (an election round), [votes for itself, and asks its peers for their votes](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raft_(algorithm)#:~:text=It%20starts%20the%20election%20by%20increasing%20the%20term%20counter%2C%20voting%20for%20itself%20as%20new%20leader%2C%20and%20sending%20a%20message%20to%20all%20other%20servers%20requesting%20their%20vote>).

[Each node votes for at most one candidate per term](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raft_(algorithm)#:~:text=A%20server%20will,new%20election%20begins.>). In this five-node cluster, a candidate needs **three votes**, including its own. Two candidates cannot both win a majority in the same term because their majorities would need to share a voter.
