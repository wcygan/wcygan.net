A [gossip protocol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gossip_protocol#:~:text=A%20gossip%20protocol%20or%20epidemic%20protocol%20is%20a%20procedure%20or%20process%20of%20computer%20peer%2Dto%2Dpeer%20communication%20that%20is%20based%20on%20the%20way%20epidemics%20spread.) spreads
information through repeated exchanges between peers. A node chooses another
node, shares what it knows, and the recipient can pass that information along.
It's similar to to [how disease spreads during an epidemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epidemic#:~:text=rapid%20spread%20of%20disease%20to%20a%20large%20number%20of%20hosts%20in%20a%20given%20population%20within%20a%20short%20period%20of%20time).

In [Cassandra](https://cassandra.apache.org/doc/stable/cassandra/architecture/overview.html), a new node can introduce itself to a seed node, who will gossip to it's peers, introducing them to the new node.

## Gossip in Cassandra

[Cassandra’s gossip documentation](https://cassandra.apache.org/doc/stable/cassandra/architecture/dynamo.html#gossip)
explains that nodes exchange endpoint state: membership, heartbeats, and other
cluster metadata. When a node joins, it contacts a seed node. Gossip then spreads
that membership information to other peers.

In the code, [`GossipTask.run`](https://github.com/apache/cassandra/blob/81b458017d8aef6e7dcd91d3f31d651bdad1aaa8/src/java/org/apache/cassandra/gms/Gossiper.java#L337-L385)
starts a gossip exchange, and
[`sendGossip`](https://github.com/apache/cassandra/blob/81b458017d8aef6e7dcd91d3f31d651bdad1aaa8/src/java/org/apache/cassandra/gms/Gossiper.java#L984-L1012)
chooses a peer to contact.
