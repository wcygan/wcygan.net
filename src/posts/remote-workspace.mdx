Go for a walk and talk to your robot over the phone.

[Rex](https://www.superlogical.com/updates/public-testing-beginning) and [ChatGPT Desktop](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/app) give you two ways to do this. Leave your project on a machine that stays running, such as a Mac mini, and connect from your phone.
