Three nodes share what they know using vector clocks. Step through two independent
client writes, then follow the messages from A → B → C.

<VectorClockDemo />

A write, a send, and a receive each count as an event. A’s counter reaches 2
because it handles one write and sends one message. The numbers are event counts,
not stored data. The dashed lines show message paths.
